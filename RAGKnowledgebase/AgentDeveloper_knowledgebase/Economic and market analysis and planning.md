# MCI-Concord Concept Planning, Economic and Market Analysis, and Proposed Zoning

**Location:** Concord, Massachusetts / Ongoing
**Consultant Lead:** Agency Landscape + Planning
**Awards:** APA-MA Medium Sized Community Planning Project Award (2025)

---

### Project Background

In January 2024, Governor Maura Healey announced the decommissioning of MCI-Concord—the state’s oldest men’s correctional facility—with plans to sell and redevelop the property. The Town of Concord and the Massachusetts Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance (DCAMM) enlisted the firm **Agency** for a 5-month "sprint" planning and visioning process. This project represents a "once-in-a-generation" opportunity to transform a site with a complex history into a place that serves and uplifts current and future communities.

---

### Planning Scenarios and Evaluation

The resulting Plan presents site scenarios created with input from residents, workers, formerly incarcerated individuals, and surrounding communities. Scenarios explored various land uses, including:

* **Open Space**
* **Community and Civic Uses**
* **Housing**
* **Commercial Development**

Each scenario was evaluated based on:

* Financial feasibility and fiscal impacts
* Infrastructure and mobility
* Density and implementation timelines

---

### Cultural and Historical Storytelling

The Plan emphasizes that centering stories is essential as the site moves beyond its historical role. It provides guidance on cultural and historical storytelling, with specific focus on:

* **Malcolm X**: Highlighting his period of incarceration at the facility.
* **Contemplative Journeys**: Outlining paths that can be woven into future development to encourage reflection.
* **Ethical Approach**: The project aims neither to erase the painful past nor to exploit the prison history as a marketing gimmick, but rather to honor the experiences of those connected to the site.
