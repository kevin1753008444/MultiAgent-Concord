# Healey-Driscoll Administration Offers 450 Acres of State-Owned Sites for Future Housing Development

**For immediate release:** 6/02/2025**Governor Maura Healey and Lt. Governor Kim Driscoll**​**Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities**

---

### Overview

The Healey-Driscoll Administration today released an inventory of more than 450 acres of surplus state-owned land that could be used for the development of 3,500 new housing units across Massachusetts. The administration expects to make 17 additional sites available to developers in the next year, including issuing Requests for Proposals (RFPs) for ten sites and holding an auction for an additional seven sites in September 2025.

Governor Maura Healey previously signed an Executive Order directing the Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC), the Executive Office for Administration and Finance (EOANF), and the Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance (DCAMM) to develop the inventory. This inventory will be used to support the administration's multi-pronged efforts to increase housing production and lower costs across the state.

### Projects Currently Underway

Several projects are already repurposing surplus state properties, with more than 1,500 units in the pipeline:

* ​**Former Boston State Hospital (BSH) Campus**​: The final phases of redevelopment will include 466 new rental and homeownership units, many of which are deed restricted affordable, across three projects: Olmsted Green, Olmsted Village, and Harvard Commons.
* ​**Former Veterans Home at Chelsea**​: Phase I will break ground toward the end of 2025, yielding the first 69 units in 2027, with a total of 241 units by 2029.
* ​**Former Department of Unemployment Assistance Building**​: Designated to New Vision Enterprise, LLC to create 35 units of new housing in downtown Brockton.
* ​**Parcel SR-25, Roxbury**​: Provisionally designated to Urbanica for 100 units of housing, retail, a satellite location for the National Center for Afro-American Artists, and a public park.
* ​**South Campus at Salem State University**​: A 23-acre parcel expected to yield over 350 units; currently in the permitting phase.

### Upcoming Solicitations and Auctions

Over the next six months, DCAMM will issue solicitations for:

* **RFPs** for vacant court buildings in **Lowell** and ​**Fitchburg**​.
* **RFPs** for sites at **Bridgewater State University** (106 Hale St.) and **Middlesex Community College** (210 Springs Rd. in Bedford).
* **Auctions in September** for parcels at the former **Westboro State Hospital** and the former ​**J.T. Berry Rehabilitation Center**​.

### Environmental and Agricultural Protections

The administration worked with the Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs (EEA) to ensure conservation land is protected. All land protected under Article 97 and agricultural preservation restrictions have been excluded from consideration.

### Future Sites (Late 2025 - 2026)

Additional sites to be made available include:
