# MCI Concord Advisory Board - Project Consultants Report

**Consultants:** Agency Landscape + Planning
**Status:** Vision Plan Completed (Spring 2025)

---

### Vision Plan Milestone

The **Vision Plan (and Appendices)** for the future of MCI Concord is now live. This milestone aims to reposition the historic prison site as a sustainable, vital district connected to:

* Its community
* Natural surroundings
* The region
* Its history

The plan was assembled collaboratively over Spring 2025, reflecting hundreds of perspectives and a broad analysis of site opportunities and constraints. Community memory and voice are integral to the vision, serving as inspiration for a future that meets current needs and aligns with community values.

---

### Advisory Board Mission

The MCI Concord Advisory Board, in partnership with local leaders and residents, is committed to ensuring development reflects the values and aspirations of the community. This is an ongoing conversation regarding the development scenarios presented to create a lasting legacy for future generations.

The project focuses on repurposing the **83-acre site** of the former correctional facility. The process aligns with community needs and state priorities, including:

* Housing
* Sustainability
* Economic development

---

### Legal and Administrative Background

The closure of MCI Concord was explicitly included in the **Fiscal Year 2025 Governor’s Budget Bill (Section 107)**. This legislation provides for the sale, lease, transfer, or disposal of the property by the Commonwealth’s **Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance (DCAMM)**.

**Purpose of the Board:**
To educate and advise the Select Board on the most effective strategy to achieve a property disposition most favorable to the interests of the Town.

---

### Organizational Structure

The Advisory Board has formed three subcommittees to advance their work:

* **Community Outreach Subcommittee**: Focuses on engaging the public.
* **Legal & Government Affairs Subcommittee**: Manages regulatory and inter-governmental relations.
* **Land Use Planning & Strategy Subcommittee**: Focuses on the physical and strategic development of the site.
