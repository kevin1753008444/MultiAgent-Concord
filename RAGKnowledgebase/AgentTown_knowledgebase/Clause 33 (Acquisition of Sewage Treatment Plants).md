# Article 33 - MCI Concord - Planning

**Article Status:** Finance Committee Hearing #3 (May 1, 2025)
**Sponsor:** Select Board
**Recommendations:** Affirmative Action by Finance Committee and Select Board

---

### Article Purpose

To determine whether the Town will raise and appropriate or transfer from available funds a sum of money to be expended under the direction of the Town Manager. This funding is designated for:

* Evaluating the feasibility of acquiring the existing **MCI Concord Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**.
* General planning purposes associated with the **MCI Concord** redevelopment.

---

### Article Explanation

In January 2024, the Governor’s FY25 Budget Plan included a provision to conditionally allow the ownership of the WWTP to be transferred to the Town of Concord for public use.

* **Facility Capacity**: The wastewater treatment facility serving MCI Concord has a design capacity of **0.31 million gallons per day (MGD)**.
* **Operational Timeline**: Prison operations ceased on June 30, 2024, and inmate housing operations concluded on July 17, 2024.
* **Negotiation Status**: On February 10, 2025, the Select Board directed the Town Manager, Director of Public Works, and Town Counsel to negotiate a **Land Disposition Agreement (LDA)** with the Division of Capital Management & Maintenance (DCAMM).

---

### Strategic Importance

Taking ownership of the facility offers a "rare opportunity" to resolve wastewater treatment capacity constraints that have impacted the Town for over 25 years. The project must balance multiple needs:

* **Economic Growth**: Meeting development interests within the community.
* **Site Redevelopment**: Serving the 51-acre site formerly occupied by MCI.
* **State Facilities**: Serving the needs of "The Farm" (Northeast Correctional Center), the State Police barracks, and the MassDOT Maintenance Facility along Route 2.

---

### Article Motion

Mr. Howell moves that the Town transfer from the **Certified Free Cash Balance of July 1, 2024**, the sum of **$250,000** for the feasibility evaluation of the WWTP acquisition and general planning for MCI Concord.
