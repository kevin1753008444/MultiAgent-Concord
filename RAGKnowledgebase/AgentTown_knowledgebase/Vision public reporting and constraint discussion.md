# MCI Concord Advisory Board Outlines Timeline, Constraints, and Public Outreach

**Date:** April 24, 2025
**Location:** Town of Concord, Middlesex County, Massachusetts
**Key Presenter:** John Cooley (Chair of the Community Outreach Subcommittee)

---

### Executive Summary

John Cooley and the MCI Concord Advisory Board briefed the West Concord Advisory Committee (WCAC) on an accelerated, consultant-led visioning process for the 51-acre MCI Concord site. The project is described as a “sprint” that must be completed by June 30, 2025, to satisfy funding timelines.

---

### Key Constraints and Technical Issues

The presentation identified three primary constraints governing the redevelopment:

1. **Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**:
    * The existing permit limits the volume of new development the town can support.
    * Proposed capacity allocation: One-third for state uses (prison farm/state police), one-third for site redevelopment, and one-third for broader town connections.
    * The facility requires modernization, with internal cost estimates reaching up to approximately $25 million.
2. **Route 2 and Rotary Configuration**:
    * MassDOT is currently re-evaluating the Route 2 rotary near the site.
    * Primary entry points and access remain negotiable and must be coordinated with MassDOT’s evolving designs.
3. **State Control**:
    * While the town uses zoning as its primary lever, the property is expected to be controlled by the Department of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance (DCAMM).
    * DCAMM is responsible for the competitive developer selection process and is not required to sell the land to the town.

---

### Planning Process and Timeline

* **Consultant Team**: The firm **Agency** (supported by subconsultants like Landwise) was selected to run community visioning and develop three development scenarios.
* **Public Outreach Schedule**:
  * Four public workshops (April 30 and May 28 identified as key dates).
  * Pop-up events (e.g., April 19) and online surveys via [mci-concord.org](http://mci-concord.org).
* **Implementation Forecast**:
  * **2026**: Rezoning proposal ready for Town Meeting.
  * **2028–2030**: Earliest projected construction start, following a year-plus of permitting and design.

---

### Scenario Modeling and Impacts

The consultants will provide modeling to evaluate the following:

* **Fiscal Effects**: Estimated tax revenues versus required public services.
* **School Enrollment**: Estimates of schoolchildren based on dwelling types and bedroom counts.
* **Traffic Generation**: Analysis of local road impacts.
* **Hypothetical Example**: A scenario involving approximately 100 housing units and 30,000 square feet of commercial space was discussed during the Q&A.

### Community Concerns (Q&A)

West Concord residents raised several priorities for the consultants to address:

* Integration with existing West Concord village businesses.
* Connectivity between the MCI parcel and the village.
* The fate and condition of historic resources on the site.
* Coordination with other town property planning (e.g., 2229 Main St).
